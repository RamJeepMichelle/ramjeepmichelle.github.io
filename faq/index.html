Can you drive on New Smyrna Beach?

Yes, in marked driving zones, tides permitting. May 1 to October 31: 8 a.m. to 7 p.m. or sundown, whichever comes first. November 1 to April 30: sunrise to sunset. No night driving.

Volusia County residents drive free after registering. Visitors pay $30 a day or $150 a year. Speed limit 10 mph. Ramps close at high tide, so check a tide chart. Full beach driving guide

Is a Jeep Wrangler a good daily driver in Florida?

Yes, if you want fun more than quiet. It rides firmer and louder than a crossover. Florida pays you back: no snow, no salted roads, the top off most of the year, the beach ten minutes away. Rinse the salt and sand off after beach runs.

Is the Jeep Wrangler 4xe worth it in Florida?

It can be, if you charge at home and drive short trips. The 4xe plug-in hybrid makes 375 horsepower and 470 lb-ft of torque. It covers roughly 20 to 30 miles on electric power depending on model year, then runs on gas. Warm, flat Florida is easy on it. No home charger, most of the benefit goes away.

Why do Jeep owners put rubber ducks on other Jeeps?

It's called Jeep ducking. Allison Parliament started it in Ontario, Canada, in July 2020. She left a rubber duck on a Jeep with a note: "Nice Jeep, have a great day." It went worldwide. Get ducked, keep it, pass one on. Never duck your own. Jeep ducking, explained
